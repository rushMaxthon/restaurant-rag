# Product Presentation Overview

> **Restaurant RAG**  
> An AI-assisted food ordering and restaurant growth platform

---

## Executive Snapshot

Restaurant RAG brings together:

- 📱 a customer mobile ordering experience
- 🤖 AI-powered discovery and personalization
- 💻 a business control panel for restaurants and operators
- 📈 engagement, offers, combos, and analytics tools

### Product Promise

| Audience | Core Value |
| --- | --- |
| 👤 Customers | Faster discovery, easier ordering, more relevant food choices |
| 🍴 Restaurants | Higher conversion, smarter upselling, stronger repeat engagement |
| 🧑‍💼 Operators | Centralized control, visibility, and business decision support |

---

## Product Vision

Restaurant RAG is designed to make food ordering feel:

- intelligent
- personalized
- guided
- business-ready

Instead of showing every user the same menu and the same promotions, the platform helps each customer discover the right restaurants, dishes, combos, and offers at the right time.

---

## Platform At a Glance

**What happens**

```text
📱 Customer Uses Mobile App
        │
        ▼
⚙️ Backend Handles Identity, Menus, Orders, and Rules
        │
        ▼
🤖 AI Enhances Search, Chat, Picks, Offers, and Combos
        │
        ▼
🗄️ Platform Uses Live Data and Business Context
        │
        ▼
⭐ Customer Receives Personalized Ordering Experience

Separately:

💻 Admin Uses Control Panel
        │
        ▼
⚙️ Backend Supports Restaurant, Menu, Offer, Order, and Analytics Management
```

### Key Highlights

- Customer journeys and business operations run on one connected platform
- AI improves relevance without replacing business control
- Restaurants and admins gain better visibility while customers get a more personal experience

### Business Benefits

- Better conversion
- Better retention opportunities
- Better operational control
