# Rider app wording: Hindi and Gujarati

Riders read this on a bike stand, in sun, between deliveries. Write the way a
rider in Surat or Ahmedabad actually talks: everyday Hindi/Gujarati, with the
English words riders already use (order, online, km, OTP-style "code") kept as
they are, written in the local script. Never textbook ("shuddh") words when a
common loan word exists. Short beats complete.

Rules:
- Keep `{name}` placeholders exactly as in English. Numbers, ₹ and "km" stay as
  they are; digits stay 0-9 (not Devanagari/Gujarati digits).
- Address the rider politely but simply: Hindi "आप" with plain verbs ("ऑर्डर लें",
  "स्लाइड करें"); Gujarati "તમે" ("ઓર્ડર લો", "સ્લાઇડ કરો").
- Button labels are 1-3 words.
- Same English word -> same translation everywhere. Use this table:
